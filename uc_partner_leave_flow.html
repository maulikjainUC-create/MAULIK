Revised partner flow

The existing home page stays intact. Leave management is added inside the hamburger menu.

Medical leave

1Select a date range and daily start/end time
2Review the hours and confirm all affected jobs will be removed
3Calendar blocks immediately; the app creates a unique leave application
4Upload a legitimate MC to that application within 48 hours
5Missed MC deadline automatically flags the leave as unpaid

Annual leave

1Select one or more full days
2Confirm the date range and remaining balance
3Calendar blocks automatically and dates are sent to the employer
There is no request tracker or Ops approval step. Medical leave can span multiple dates; annual leave remains full-day only.

Backend calendar operations

1Every submission creates a structured leave record with provider ID, leave type, dates, hours and MC status.
AAutomatic route: the backend sends the selected dates and hours to the scheduling service and blocks the provider calendar immediately.
BOps-assisted route: the same data is sent to an Ops queue, report or form-equivalent feed. Ops agents manually block the correct calendars and mark the record complete.
Today, Ops agents act on Google Form entries. The partner-app record can replace that form, or feed the same manual workflow during rollout. Automatic blocking and manual Ops blocking can coexist as primary and fallback paths.

Weekly / monthly employer reporting

1Aggregate leave records by provider and reporting period.
2Send the employer a weekly or monthly view of MCs submitted, medical-leave days or hours, annual-leave days and unpaid-leave flags.
3Include opening entitlement, leave used and remaining balance for each provider.
Suggested report fields: provider name/ID, employer, leave application ID, leave type, dates, hours or days, MC upload status, paid/unpaid status, calendar-block status and remaining entitlement.
