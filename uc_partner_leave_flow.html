Revised partner flow

The existing home page stays intact. Leave management is added inside the hamburger menu.

Simplified medical leave

1Show the current date and ask only: Today or Tomorrow?
2Choose A few hours, One full day, or More than one day.
3For hours, choose start and end time from simple dropdowns. For one day, no time entry is needed.
4Show the exact period and number of affected jobs before any calendar change.
5One day or less blocks automatically after confirmation. More than one day creates a Help Centre ticket and does not block immediately.
6Upload a legitimate MC within 48 hours; missed deadline flags the leave as unpaid.

Annual leave

1Select one or more full days
2Confirm the date range and remaining balance
3Calendar blocks automatically and dates are sent to the employer
The medical-leave guardrail is different: one day or less may auto-block, while longer MC requests require Help Centre review.

Backend calendar operations

1Every submission creates a structured leave record with provider ID, leave type, dates, hours and MC status.
AAutomatic route: one day or less is sent to the scheduling service only after the partner confirms the exact impact.
BReview route: more than one day creates a Help Centre ticket. Ops or support evaluates the request before blocking the calendar.
The partner app can replace the Google Form or feed the same manual Ops workflow during rollout. No jobs are removed merely because the user selects an option.

Weekly / monthly employer reporting

1Aggregate leave records by provider and reporting period.
2Send the employer a weekly or monthly view of MCs submitted, medical-leave days or hours, annual-leave days and unpaid-leave flags.
3Include opening entitlement, leave used and remaining balance for each provider.
Suggested report fields: provider name/ID, employer, leave application ID, leave type, dates, hours or days, MC upload status, paid/unpaid status, calendar-block status and remaining entitlement.
